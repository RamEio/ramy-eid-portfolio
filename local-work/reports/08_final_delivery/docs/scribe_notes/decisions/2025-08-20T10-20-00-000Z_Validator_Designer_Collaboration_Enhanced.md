## Decision Summary

Enhanced the collaboration between **Validator** and **Designer** agents to establish a close working relationship where user research and design are inherently interconnected, ensuring all design decisions are grounded in authentic user validation.

### **🎨 Close Collaboration Rationale**

- **Design Adaptation:** Designer adjusts designs based on validation feedback

- **Research Phase Collaboration:** Joint user research and persona development
- **Design Iteration Collaboration:** Continuous feedback loop between design and validation
- **Design Validation Integration:** Real-time user perspective during design process
- **Collaborative Deliverables:** Shared documentation and success metrics
