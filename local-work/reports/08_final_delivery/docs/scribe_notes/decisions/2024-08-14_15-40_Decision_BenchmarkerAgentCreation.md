# 📝 Decision - Benchmarker Agent Creation
**Date:** 2024-08-14 15:40  
**Agent:** Counselor  
**Category:** Decision  

## 🎯 Summary
Decision to create a new Benchmarker agent to conduct comprehensive competitive analysis and market research across multiple platforms, providing valuable insights to support design decisions and product strategy.

## 📋 Details
The client requested the addition of a new agent called "Benchmarker" that will work closely with the Designer and Product Manager agents. This agent will conduct thorough benchmarking and competitive analysis across various platforms and resources to inform design decisions and product strategy.

**Benchmarker Agent Capabilities:**

**Platform-Specific Research:**
- **Mobile Apps:** Mobbin, Dribbble, Behance, App Store/Google Play, Product Hunt, Sensor Tower
- **Web Applications:** Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, The FWA, Behance, Dribbble, Product Hunt
- **Software/Desktop:** Dribbble, Behance, Product Hunt, GitHub, AlternativeTo, G2/Capterra

**Research Focus Areas:**
- User interface patterns and design trends
- Feature sets and functionality comparisons
- User experience flows and interaction patterns
- Market positioning and competitive advantages
- Pricing strategies and monetization models
- User feedback and satisfaction metrics

**Deliverables:**
- Comprehensive benchmark reports
- Competitive analysis matrices
- Design inspiration databases
- Trend analysis reports
- Market positioning insights

## 🔄 Impact
This decision enhances the project's research and competitive analysis capabilities:
- **Informed Design Decisions:** Designer receives current market trends and competitor analysis
- **Strategic Product Planning:** Product Manager gets competitive insights for RICE prioritization
- **Market Positioning:** Better understanding of competitive landscape and opportunities
- **Design Inspiration:** Access to current design patterns and best practices
- **Risk Mitigation:** Identification of competitive threats and market gaps
