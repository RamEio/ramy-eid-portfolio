About Me

With over 10 years of experience in UX/Product Design, I specialize in retail innovation and mobile app design. My background in cognitive engineering (ENSC Bordeaux) and experience at major companies like Decathlon, Carrefour, Leroy Merlin, and Capgemini has equipped me with expertise in design sprints, user research, and data-driven UX optimization.

I lead product vision alignment, continuous discovery processes, and systematic UX testing. My work spans from accessibility-focused design sprints to international PIM systems, always with a focus on user-centered design and measurable business impact.
