## 🎯 Summary

## 📋 Details
The Product Manager agent has been significantly enhanced to act as a **guardian of the global vision**, continuously monitoring and validating that all project decisions, technical choices, and design directions remain aligned with core project objectives.

**Key Enhancements Added:**

1. **Strategic Guardian Functions:**
   - **Vision Alignment Monitoring:** Continuous monitoring of all project decisions for strategic alignment
   - **Strategic Coherence Validation:** Validation of each phase and decision against core objectives
   - **Scope Creep Prevention:** Identification and prevention of scope creep
   - **Cross-Agent Alignment:** Ensuring all agents' deliverables align with overall strategy
   - **Strategic Risk Assessment:** Evaluation of strategic risks and potential misalignments

2. **Enhanced Workflow:**
   - **Continuous Vision Monitoring:** Monitors all project phases and agent deliverables
   - **Strategic Validation Gates:** Reviews major decisions against core objectives
   - **Alignment Feedback:** Provides strategic feedback to agents when needed
   - **Risk Assessment:** Continuously assesses strategic risks
   - **Scope Control:** Prevents scope creep by ensuring additions serve core vision
   - **Strategic Reporting:** Reports alignment status to Counselor at milestones

3. **Enhanced Communication:**
   - **Vision Alignment Alerts:** Identifies potential misalignments in technical or design choices
   - **Scope Creep Warnings:** Prevents unnecessary additions that don't serve core objectives
   - **Strategic Risk Assessments:** Evaluates design and technical decisions against target audience needs
   - **Cross-Agent Alignment:** Ensures consistency between all agent deliverables

4. **Enhanced Quality Criteria:**
   - **Vision Coherence:** All decisions maintain alignment with core objectives
   - **Strategic Validation:** Every major decision validated against global vision
   - **Risk Awareness:** Proactive identification of strategic risks
   - **Scope Discipline:** Maintenance of project scope without unnecessary additions
   - **Cross-Agent Consistency:** Consistency across all agent deliverables
