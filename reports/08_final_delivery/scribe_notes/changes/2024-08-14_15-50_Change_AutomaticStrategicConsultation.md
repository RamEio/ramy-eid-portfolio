# 📝 Change - Automatic Strategic Consultation Implementation
**Date:** 2024-08-14 15:50  
**Agent:** Counselor  
**Category:** Change  

## 🎯 Summary
Implemented automatic strategic consultation protocol ensuring the Product Manager is automatically consulted by the Counselor for all major decisions, technical choices, and design directions before proceeding to the next phase.

## 📋 Details
The Counselor agent has been enhanced with an **automatic strategic consultation protocol** that ensures the Product Manager is automatically consulted for strategic validation of all major project decisions. This automation guarantees that no significant decision is made without strategic guardian oversight.

**Key Automation Features:**

1. **Mandatory Consultation Points:**
   - **Design Phase:** Before validating wireframes and design deliverables
   - **Technical Decisions:** Before approving technical stack and architecture choices
   - **Feature Additions:** Before adding any new features or scope changes
   - **Agent Deliverables:** Before validating any agent's major deliverables
   - **Project Milestones:** At each major project milestone and phase transition
   - **Risk Assessment:** Before proceeding when strategic risks are identified
   - **Scope Changes:** Before approving any modifications to project scope
   - **Resource Allocation:** Before making significant resource or timeline decisions

2. **Automatic Consultation Process:**
   - **Trigger Identification:** Counselor automatically identifies decision points requiring strategic validation
   - **Automatic Consultation:** Counselor automatically consults Product Manager without manual intervention
   - **Strategic Validation:** Product Manager validates alignment with global vision and core objectives
   - **Feedback Integration:** Counselor incorporates strategic validation before proceeding
   - **Documentation:** Scribe automatically documents the strategic consultation and outcome

3. **Automatic Consultation Triggers:**
   - Any major decision that could impact project scope or direction
   - Technical stack changes or architecture modifications
   - Feature additions or scope modifications
   - Design direction changes or significant UI/UX decisions
   - Resource allocation or timeline modifications
   - Risk identification or mitigation strategies
   - Quality standard changes or acceptance criteria modifications
   - Cross-agent conflicts or alignment issues

## 🔄 Impact
This automation ensures:
- **Guaranteed Strategic Oversight:** No major decision is made without Product Manager validation
- **Consistent Alignment:** All project decisions maintain alignment with core objectives
- **Risk Prevention:** Early identification and prevention of strategic misalignments
- **Quality Assurance:** Higher quality deliverables through mandatory strategic validation
- **Scope Control:** Automatic prevention of scope creep through strategic consultation
- **Project Success:** Better alignment with business objectives through automated oversight
