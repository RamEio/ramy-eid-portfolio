Frequently Asked Questions

What is Starter Hub?

Starter Hub is a simple web interface that makes it easy for anyone to create complete, deployable multi-agent systems using popular AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini Pro, or Claude. No coding or technical knowledge required!

How does it work?

Simply choose your AI assistant, copy the agent, paste it into your AI assistant, and follow the guided discovery process. In under 10 minutes, you'll have a complete multi-agent system ready to deploy.

What do I get?

You get a complete multi-agent system with 15 specialized agents, full project structure, deployment configurations, documentation, and a ready-to-deploy website - everything you need to run your own AI agency.

Is it really free?

Yes! Starter Hub is completely free to use. You only need access to an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Gemini Pro, or Claude) which may have their own usage costs.

What types of projects can I create?

Any type! Web projects, mobile apps, SaaS applications, e-commerce stores, educational platforms, design systems, and more. The system adapts to your specific needs.

Do I need technical skills?

No! Starter Hub is designed for complete beginners. The process is copy-paste simple, and the generated system handles all the technical complexity for you.

How long does it take?

The entire process takes about 10 minutes: 8 minutes for the discovery process, and 2 minutes to receive your complete system.

Can I customize the generated system?

Absolutely! The system is fully customizable. You can modify colors, content, features, and functionality to match your specific needs and brand.

What if something goes wrong?

If you encounter issues, check this help page first. If you still need assistance, you can contact support or ask questions in the community.

Is my data secure?

Yes! Starter Hub doesn't store any of your data. The entire process happens in your AI assistant, and your project is created locally on your system.

Can I use it for commercial projects?

Yes! The systems you create with Starter Hub are yours to use for any purpose, including commercial projects and businesses.

What if I need help after creating my system?

The generated system includes comprehensive documentation and support resources. You can also reach out to the community for additional help.

Troubleshooting

Copy button doesn't work

Solution: Try refreshing the page or use the manual copy method: right-click on the agent file and select "Copy" or use Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac).

AI assistant doesn't respond

Solution: Make sure you've pasted the entire agent content and said "Act as the Project Starter Agent" exactly as shown.

Process takes too long

Solution: The discovery process should take about 8 minutes. If it's taking longer, try being more specific in your answers to speed up the process.

Generated system doesn't work

Solution: Check that all files were created properly. If files are missing, try the process again or contact support.

Deployment issues

Solution: The generated system includes deployment configurations for Netlify and Vercel. Follow the included deployment guide for step-by-step instructions.

Browser compatibility

Solution: Starter Hub works best with modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Update your browser if you're having issues.
