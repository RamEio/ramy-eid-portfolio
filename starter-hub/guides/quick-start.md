## 🚀 Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Choose Your AI Assistant

Select from the available options:
- **ChatGPT** - Most popular AI assistant
- **Gemini Pro** - Google's powerful AI
- **Claude** - Anthropic's AI assistant

### Step 2: Copy the Agent

1. Click the "Copy Agent" button for your chosen AI assistant
2. The agent will be copied to your clipboard
3. You'll see a confirmation message

### Step 3: Open Your AI Assistant

1. Go to your chosen AI assistant:
   - **ChatGPT:** [chat.openai.com](https://chat.openai.com)
   - **Gemini Pro:** [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
   - **Claude:** [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)

### Step 4: Paste and Activate

1. Paste the agent content into your AI assistant
2. Say: **"Act as the Project Starter Agent"**
3. The agent will introduce itself and begin the discovery process

### Step 5: Follow the Discovery Process

The agent will guide you through a structured 8-minute discovery process:

#### Phase 1: Project Foundation (2 minutes)
- Project type identification
- Scope definition

#### Phase 2: Technical Requirements (3 minutes)
- Technology preferences
- Integration needs

#### Phase 3: User Experience (2 minutes)
- User journey mapping
- Success criteria

#### Phase 4: Validation & Confirmation (1 minute)
- Requirement summary
- Final confirmation

### Step 6: Receive Your Complete System

After the discovery process, you'll receive:
- Complete project structure
- All 15 agent files
- Deployment configurations
- Documentation
- Ready-to-deploy website

## 🛠️ Tips for Success

### Before Starting
1. **Have a clear project idea** - Know what you want to create
2. **Set aside 10 minutes** - Complete the process without interruption
3. **Be specific** - The more details you provide, the better the result

### During the Process
1. **Answer all questions** - Don't skip any discovery steps
2. **Be honest about constraints** - Budget, timeline, technical skills
3. **Ask for clarification** - If something isn't clear, ask!

### After Completion

2. **Customize as needed** - Modify colors, content, features
3. **Deploy immediately** - Get your project online right away
